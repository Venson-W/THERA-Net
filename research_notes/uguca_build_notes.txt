Scientific scope:
This is the full causal elastodynamic spectral boundary-integral implementation,
including its time-history kernels, not a quasidynamic radiation-damping model.
The TPV101 symmetric shear interface enforces zero opening and transverse slip;
it solves a scalar strike-slip law on a two-dimensional fault surface in 3D
elastic space. Dumped top_disp_0 and top_velo_0 must be multiplied by two for
relative fault displacement and velocity. Arrival extraction must explicitly
mask sites which never cross threshold. Native helper argmax alone does not.
Production driver tracks first crossing every integration step at 1e-3 m/s and
0.5 m/s and saves the unruptured sentinel -1. A mesh refinement study is required
before asserting resolved rupture-front accuracy; matching NN/reference on one
coarse grid does not establish continuum convergence or independent validation.

Numerical near-rest qualification:
The nonlinear solve uses a traction residual tolerance of 1e-7+1e-13*abs(S) Pa.
The reconstructed near-rest speed consequently has limited relative accuracy,
even though the absolute error is tiny. At t=0 on the principal grids, actual
reference speeds are approximately (0.838--0.854)e-12 m/s and neural speeds
(0.996--1.005)e-12 m/s. The minimum computed local power can be slightly
negative from scalar-solve tolerance plus cancellation; no clipping is applied.
This does not establish a strict floating-point global SBI energy certificate.
